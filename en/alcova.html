The Alcova is a little nest on the first floor overlooking the life of the Borgo's square: spaces cared for in every detail, perfect for a couple or a small family looking for an elegant, unhurried holiday.

The covered veranda is its star, with a dining area, sun loungers and a barbecue: breakfasts in the sun, dinners with a view of the Borgo's square coming to life below you, and loungers for the hottest hours.

Inside, a double bedroom and a sofa bed in the living area. The kitchen is fully equipped, with an induction hob, oven and dishwasher, and the bathroom houses the washing machine. Everything within reach, nothing superfluous.

Parking in the park is free and unreserved. And for those who want to be at the centre of everything without giving up tranquillity, the sea is close by.
