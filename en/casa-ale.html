Casa Ale is the retreat for those who want the privacy of a house all to themselves: cosy spaces with a corner garden that seems made for slow days.

The corner garden has a dining area and sun loungers waiting for a rest after a long day by the sea, while the covered veranda with its dining table gives shade in the hottest hours. In the evening the brick-built barbecue turns every dinner into a little ritual, and the outdoor shower cools you off when you get back.

Inside, the double bedroom and the room with two single beds, together with the sofa bed in the living room with kitchen, comfortably sleep up to 6 guests: ideal for a family or two couples. The bathroom is practical and the washing machine makes everything even easier.

Garden, shade and quiet. Parking in the park is free and unreserved, and the sea is very close: just a short stroll away.
