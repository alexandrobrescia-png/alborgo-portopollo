Casa Lory is for those who dream of a holiday lived outdoors: bright, well-kept spaces designed for families who want to be together without giving up their own quiet moments.

The heart of the house is outside: two gardens, with a shaded dining area and a lounge area where the day stretches into the late evening. The covered veranda with its dining table is always ready for breakfasts and dinners in the open air, and the brick-built barbecue invites slow dinners with the scent of embers and good conversation. The double bedroom has its own private garden: a little retreat for the morning coffee.

Inside, the living area with kitchen and sofa bed is an invitation to gather, while the double bedroom and the room with two single beds strike the right balance between sociability and privacy. Two bathrooms for maximum comfort.

After a day by the sea, the outdoor shower is a caress of cool water before sunset. Parking in the park is free and unreserved, and the sea is just a short walk away.
