Welcome

Welcome to the holiday homes “Al Borgo”: three houses set in the Mediterranean scrub of the Borgo of Porto Pollo, just 300 metres from the sea. Here the wind carries the scent of myrtle and rosemary, the sea changes colour by the hour, from the turquoise of morning to the gold of sunset, and time follows the rhythm of an unhurried holiday. A few steps away, the Baia dei Delfini awaits with its pale sand and turquoise water, perfect for long days by the sea.

Three houses, each with its own character and carefully chosen details: Casa Lory and Casa Ale, with a garden and veranda for eating outdoors, and Alcova on the square, with its covered terrace overlooking the main square. A perfect balance between privacy and conviviality, for families, friends and young couples.

After a day among beaches, excursions to Palau and the La Maddalena Archipelago and villages to discover, you come home to an outdoor shower and a barbecue dinner. The green of the scrub blends with the blue of the sky and the sea on the horizon, with views that stay with you.

Al Borgo is more than a holiday: it is a way of experiencing Sardinia at its most authentic.

Porto Pollo and the Borgo

Porto Pollo is one of the best-loved places in Gallura for its sea and its wind, which make it a hotspot for windsurfing and kitesurfing. From here you can reach Palau and the La Maddalena Archipelago in a few minutes, as well as the beaches and coves of Gallura and the Costa Smeralda.

Our houses are in the Borgo, a residential holiday village of Sardinian stone houses, set in the Mediterranean scrub and 300 metres from the sea.

Porto Pollo

Baia dei Delfini

Just a short walk from our houses lies the Baia dei Delfini (Dolphin Bay): a long beach of pale sand with turquoise-tinted water, natural dunes crossed by wooden walkways and the outline of the La Maddalena Archipelago on the horizon. The steady wind makes it a favourite with windsurfers and kitesurfers, but it is also a perfect place to relax, swim and watch the sunset.
