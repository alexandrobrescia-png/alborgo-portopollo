Privacy policy

Data controller

The controller of your personal data is Alessandro Brescia, who manages the holiday homes “Al Borgo” (Casa Lory, Casa Ale and Alcova on the square) in Porto Pollo, Sardinia.

Contact: email Alexandro.brescia@gmail.com, phone +39 393 209 4189.

What data we process

  • The data you enter in the contact form: full name, email, phone (optional), house of interest, arrival and departure dates, number of guests and message.
  • If you write to us on WhatsApp: your phone number and the content of your messages.
  • Technical browsing data (for example the IP address) recorded in the logs of the service that hosts the website, needed for it to work and for security.

Why we process it and on what basis

We process your data to reply to your enquiries about information and availability and, if you decide to book, to follow up on the booking. The legal basis is the performance of pre-contractual measures taken at your request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). If a booking is confirmed, some data may also be processed to comply with legal obligations (for example tax obligations and reporting guests to the authorities).

We do not use your data for marketing or profiling, and we do not sell it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to reply and, if no booking follows, for no more than 12 months. For confirmed bookings we keep the data for as long as the law requires.

Who can see the data

Only the controller has access to the data. To run the website we use providers that process data on our behalf: the service that forwards the contact form to our email (Web3Forms), the service that hosts the website and, if you choose to write to us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp (Meta), which processes data under its own privacy policy. Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards required by the GDPR.

Cookies and tracking tools

This website does not use profiling cookies or analytics or advertising tools. To display its typeface it loads the Cormorant Garamond and Jost fonts from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Limited), which may therefore receive your IP address. On the Book page, to show the dates that are already taken, the site contacts a Google service (Google Apps Script) that reads the houses' calendars: here too Google may receive your IP address, but it does not receive the data you enter in the form. It uses no tracking scripts. For this reason it does not show a cookie banner.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing, object to its processing and, where applicable, receive a copy in a portable format. Write to Alexandro.brescia@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it) or with the authority of your country.

Providing your data

Providing your data is optional, but without your name, contact details and dates we cannot reply to your request.
